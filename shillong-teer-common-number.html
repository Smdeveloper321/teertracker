How Teer Common Numbers Are Calculated (Formulas Explained)

Unlike random lottery draws, Khasi archery clubs follow consistent physical parameters. Veteran analysts utilize three primary mathematical calculations:

Formula 1: The Previous Day Sum Method

Add the First Round and Second Round numbers of the preceding day:

Yesterday F/R: 25 | Yesterday S/R: 47
Sum: 25 + 47 = 72
Take the digits: 7 and 2 → These form today's primary House targets (House 7 and House 2).

Formula 2: Date Value Subtraction

Subtract today's calendar date value from the highest winning round of the week to calculate ending variance:

Week High: 85 | Date: 09
Calculation: 85 - 09 = 76
Ending value 6 or its mirror digit 1 (adding 5) indicates an active units target.

Formula 3: The 30-Day Ending Frequency Cycle

Track all ten ending digits (0–9) across the past 30 days using our Previous Results Archive. Digits with fewer than two appearances over 20 consecutive days present higher statistical probability for mean reversion.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time are daily common numbers published?

Daily common numbers are updated every morning by 9:00 AM IST, hours before the afternoon archery rounds commence.

Can I use these common numbers for Khanapara Teer?

While Khanapara draws follow similar mechanics, Khanapara has its own distinct club archery cycle. Check our Khanapara Teer Result page for dedicated Assam targets.

What is a House in Teer betting?

A House represents the first digit (tens place) of the two-digit winning score. Buying House 2 covers every number from 20 to 29.
