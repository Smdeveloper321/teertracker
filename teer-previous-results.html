How to Read and Analyze Teer Historical Charts

Studying past winning scores is the foundational method for deriving daily House and Ending predictions. Analysts look for specific statistical indicators:

  • Ending Frequency (Units Digit): Tracking how many times digits 0 through 9 have appeared as the winning second digit over a rolling 30-day window. If Ending 7 has not hit in 12 consecutive draws, analysts flag it as an overdue target.
  • House Clustering (Tens Digit): Grouping draws by tens. When House 4 appears repeatedly over three consecutive draws, probability distribution suggests variance will soon shift to opposite clusters (such as House 8 or 9).
  • Sum of Rounds: Adding the First Round number to the Second Round number. If F/R is 25 and S/R is 47, the sum is 72. The units digit of that sum (2) often forms next day's primary House.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are past Teer results updated in real time?

Yes. As soon as the afternoon draws conclude at 3:30 PM and 4:30 PM, today's numbers are immediately appended to our verified digital archive.

Can past numbers repeat?

Yes. While identical two-digit direct numbers occasionally repeat across consecutive days, repeating the exact Forecast pair (both F/R and S/R) remains rare given the 1 in 10,000 statistical probability.
