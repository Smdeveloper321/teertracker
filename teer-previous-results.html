Khasi Hills Archery Sports Association (KHASA) Shooting Schedule & Official Off-Days

KHASA Official Schedule

Under the Meghalaya Amusement and Betting Tax (Amendment) Act, traditional archery shoots operate strictly 6 days a week from Monday through Saturday. No official games are hosted on Sundays. In addition, KHASA observes declared state gazetted holidays, national commemorative dates, and indigenous Khasi cultural festivals.

How to Analyze Shillong Teer Previous Results

Archived draw charts provide objective data for identifying physical shooting trends at Polo Ground:

  1. House Rotation Tracking: Tens digits (Houses 0 through 9) alternate in clusters. When House 2 appears on consecutive days, related complement houses (such as House 7) frequently follow in the subsequent cycle.
  2. Complementary Pair Repetition: Results sharing digit sums often pair across consecutive rounds. For instance, winning number 25 (digit sum 7) frequently correlates with complement numbers such as 34, 52, or 70.
  3. First Round to Second Round Deviation: Statistical logs indicate that the Second Round number deviates from the First Round number by an average spread of 22 numerical points.
