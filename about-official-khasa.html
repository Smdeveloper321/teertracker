Legal Framework & Meghalaya State Statutes

Unlike unauthorized lottery operations elsewhere in India, Shillong Teer operates under rigorous state legislative oversight in Meghalaya. The activity is governed by three primary legal instruments:

  1. Meghalaya Societies Registration Act, 1983: KHASA was registered as a formal sports society (Registration No. SR/KHASI/29/82 of 1982), authorizing the association to administer archery competitions and preserve Khasi cultural archery heritage.
  2. Meghalaya Amusements and Betting Tax Act, 1982 (Act No. 6 of 1982): This statutory enactment legalized bookmaking on traditional archery games and instituted government tax levies on counter gross receipts, generating state public revenue.
  3. Meghalaya Regulation of the Game of Arrow Shooting and the Sale of Teer Tickets Act, 2018: Passed by the Meghalaya Legislative Assembly to modernize operations, this Act formalized strict operational rules:
    • Mandatory counter licensing with geographical spacing restrictions.
    • Strict prohibition of ticket sales to minors under 18 years of age.
    • Standardized shooting equipment, fixed distances (45 to 50 meters), and certified counting marshals.
    • Dispute arbitration procedures administered under the Office of the Deputy Commissioner.

Authorized Teer Counters: Identification & Rules

Licensed Teer booking counters operate across authorized commercial zones in Shillong (including Khyndailad / Police Bazar, Iewduh / Bara Bazar, and Polo Commercial Complex) and throughout East Khasi Hills. Legitimate operations comply with standard guidelines:

Counter Validation Checklist

  • Current district administration permit displayed on premises.
  • Receipts issued with printed counter code, serial ID, and draw date.
  • Tickets close precisely at 3:15 PM for First Round and 4:15 PM for Second Round.

Payout & Claim Windows

  • Single round winning slips pay at standard 80:1 ratio (¥1 returns ¥80).
  • Forecast double hit combinations pay up to 4,000:1 ratio.
  • Winning claims must be redeemed within 30 calendar days at the issuing counter.

KHASA & Governance: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Khasi Hills Archery Sports Association (KHASA)?

KHASA is the apex governing association comprising 12 traditional indigenous archery clubs in the Khasi Hills of Meghalaya. Established and registered in 1982, KHASA organizes the daily legal archery shooting matches at Polo Ground, Shillong.

Where is the official Shillong Teer shooting conducted?

Official shooting occurs at Polo Ground (4th Furlong, Polo Hills, McCabe Road area), Shillong, East Khasi Hills District, Meghalaya 793001.

Under which law is Shillong Teer legal in Meghalaya?

Shillong Teer is legal under the Meghalaya Amusements and Betting Tax Act 1982 and the Meghalaya Regulation of the Game of Arrow Shooting and the Sale of Teer Tickets Act 2018.

How many archery clubs participate in Shillong Teer?

12 registered sports clubs belong to KHASA, including Wahingdoh Sports Club, Malki Sports Club, Laban Sports Club, Mawlai Sports Club, Jaiaw Sports Club, and Mawkhar Sports Club.

How do authorized Teer counters operate in Meghalaya?

Licensed counters are issued formal permits by the Deputy Commissioner, East Khasi Hills. Each counter must display its government license number, issue printed or stamped serialized receipts, and restrict sales to individuals aged 18 and above.
