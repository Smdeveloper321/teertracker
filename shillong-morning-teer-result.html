Morning Session Mechanics & Target Distance

The morning archery round operates under unique conditions that differ from the afternoon Polo Ground tournament. Shooters gather early at authorized ranges in East Khasi Hills before morning winds intensify.

A team of 30 registered archers participates in the morning draw. Each shooter receives 30 traditional cane arrows for the First Round at 10:30 AM, and 20 arrows for the Second Round at 11:30 AM. The target sits at a standard distance of 45 to 50 yards. Morning dew and lower ambient temperatures often affect arrow velocity, creating distinct hit patterns that seasoned observers track daily.

Morning House and Ending Calculation Method

Many local participants apply a morning arithmetic formula to identify potential house and ending targets. The formula uses the previous day's morning result:

  • Step 1: Take the previous First Round result (for example, 53) and Second Round result (82).
  • Step 2: Add the two values: 53 + 82 = 135. The tens digit (3) and units digit (5) indicate primary house targets.
  • Step 3: Calculate the difference: |82 - 53| = 29. The digits 2 and 9 indicate potential ending values.

Remember that every archery round produces an independent physical count. Mathematical formulas serve as analytical reference points rather than deterministic outcomes.

Khanapara Morning vs Shillong Morning Teer

Players frequently confuse Shillong Morning Teer with Khanapara Morning Teer. While both announce results around the same morning window (10:30 AM and 11:30 AM), they are distinct events held at separate venues. Shillong Morning takes place within the Khasi Hills jurisdiction, while Khanapara Morning occurs near the Ri-Bhoi district border with Assam. Official scoreboards treat them as independent records.

Anti-Fraud Advisory for Morning Draws

Beware of fraudulent social media groups on Telegram and Facebook claiming to provide "confirmed morning counter leak numbers" for an upfront fee. Authorized Meghalaya booking counters strictly prohibit advance number sales. The winning number is physically determined only after the arrows hit the target and officials complete the manual count in front of public witnesses.

Morning Teer: Frequently Asked Questions

What time is the Shillong Morning Teer result declared?

The First Round (FR) is declared at 10:30 AM IST, and the Second Round (SR) is declared at 11:30 AM IST from Monday to Saturday.

What is Shillong Morning Teer?

Morning Teer is the early session of traditional Meghalaya archery shooting, opening daily wagering before the standard afternoon Polo Ground session.

How is the Morning Teer winning number determined?

Target marshals count the bamboo arrows hitting the circular target at the end of the round. The last two digits of that total arrow count form the winning number.

Are Morning Teer counters open on Sundays?

No. All licensed Teer booking counters and archery ranges throughout Meghalaya remain closed on Sundays.

What is the payout for Morning Teer?

Single round direct bets pay standard 80:1 returns (¥1 returns ¥80), while pair forecast combinations pay up to 4,000:1.
