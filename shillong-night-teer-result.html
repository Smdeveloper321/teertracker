Knight Arrow Nocturnal Target Setup & Rules

Shillong Night Teer, widely recognized across regional betting circles as Knight Arrow, provides an evening competitive archery tournament. While daytime tournaments rely on natural sunlight across open fields, the night archery range uses high-powered halogen floodlights focused directly on the circular bamboo target.

The shooting line accommodates 30 seasoned archers selected by traditional clubs. Each archer releases 30 cane arrows in the First Round at 10:30 PM IST, and 20 arrows in the Second Round at 11:30 PM IST. Because nighttime temperatures drop significantly in the Khasi Hills, cold mountain air creates denser air resistance. Archers compensate by altering their draw weight and arrow elevation.

Night Teer House & Ending Calculation Strategy

Experienced analysts use evening variance formulas to evaluate target numbers for night games:

  • House Identification: Sum the digits of the previous night's First Round. If the First Round was 19, the sum is 1 + 9 = 10, indicating House 1 and House 0.
  • Ending Identification: Subtract the smaller round number from the larger round number. If the results were 19 and 63, the difference is 63 - 19 = 44, suggesting Ending 4.
  • Forecast Pairing: Combining identified houses and endings produces direct target pairs such as 14 and 04.

Keep in mind that statistical models do not alter physical arrow flight. Every night match produces independent counts based on physical hits on the target face.

Verification and Counter Payout Deadlines

Because Night Teer concludes late in the evening, winning tickets can be redeemed at licensed booking counters the following morning between 9:00 AM and 1:00 PM. Always safeguard your physical paper slip issued by licensed agents. Counter operators verify ticket serial numbers against the official tally sheets before disbursing winnings.

Consumer Security Advisory

Night Teer attracts numerous unlicensed online websites promising guaranteed fixed numbers for the 10:30 PM draw. KHASA and legitimate archery associations have no affiliation with online booking apps. Wagering is permitted only through licensed physical retail stalls operating within Meghalaya boundaries.

Night Teer: Frequently Asked Questions

What time is the Shillong Night Teer result declared?

The First Round (FR) is declared at 10:30 PM IST, and the Second Round (SR) is declared at 11:30 PM IST from Monday to Saturday.

How does Shillong Night Teer differ from the afternoon game?

Both games follow identical counting rules based on the last two digits of arrows hitting the target, but Night Teer features an evening shooting window (10:30 PM and 11:30 PM) following separate counter ticket bookings.

Where can I find Shillong Night Teer common numbers?

Night common numbers are calculated daily by summing the day draw and night previous round numbers. Visit our dedicated Common Numbers section for verified calculations.

Is Shillong Night Teer held on Sundays?

No. All Teer shooting matches in Meghalaya are strictly closed on Sundays and designated public holidays.

What is the payout for Shillong Night Teer?

Standard licensed counter rates offer an 80:1 payout for a single round hit and up to 4,000:1 for a Forecast pair hit predicting both night rounds.
