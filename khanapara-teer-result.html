KP Teer Result (Khanapara Teer Khela) & Official Counter Rules

Commonly searched as KP Teer result or Khanapara Teer Khela by local participants across Guwahati and Ri-Bhoi, the match is determined by licensed archers shooting at regulated ranges.

Notice Regarding Khanapara Teer Facebook Groups: Multiple unverified social media pages and Facebook groups (often titled Khanapara Teer Facebook or Khanapara Teer Result Facebook) solicit online payments for "100% fixed VIP numbers". All official Teer games operate strictly via physical government-licensed counters in Meghalaya. Arrow counts cannot be predicted or manipulated online.

How Khanapara Teer Operates

Khanapara Teer (also referred to as Assam Teer) takes place at Them Marwet in Khanapara, situated along the border of Ri-Bhoi district (Meghalaya) and the capital city of Guwahati (Assam). Because the venue is located directly beside Assam's commercial entrance, Khanapara draws immense daily participation across both states.

The game follows strict traditional archery guidelines overseen by local archery committees:

  • Venue: Them Marwet Ground, Khanapara.
  • Timings: First Round shooting begins at 4:10 PM IST. Second Round shooting starts at 4:45 PM IST.
  • Arrows Fired: Licensed archers shoot bamboo arrows at a circular target. Round 1 features 30 arrows per archer, while Round 2 features 20 arrows per archer.
  • Result Calculation: Officials count all embedded arrows. The last two digits of the total count determine the winning number. For example, if 806 arrows hit the target in the first round, the winning number is 06.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time is the Khanapara Teer result declared?

The First Round is declared at 4:10 PM IST, and the Second Round is declared at 4:45 PM IST, Monday through Saturday.

Can I check Khanapara Morning Teer results here?

Yes, Khanapara Morning Teer draws at 11:30 AM (F/R) and 12:15 PM (S/R) and is tracked live in our session section above.

Are Khanapara and Guwahati Teer the same game?

Yes. Because Khanapara sits directly on the border of Guwahati, local players commonly call it Guwahati Teer or Assam Teer.

Is there an official app for Khanapara Teer?

No single government app exists. Independent informational sites like TeerTracker publish live numbers directly from local ground tally counters.
